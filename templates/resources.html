Before a Flood / Disaster

  • Know your area's flood and cyclone risk level
  • Elevate critical utilities and emergency supplies
  • Establish an emergency family communication plan
  • Prepare a portable 72-hour emergency kit
  • Subscribe to CrisisAware early warning alerts

During a Disaster

  • Move to designated high-ground shelters immediately
  • Avoid walking, swimming, or driving through moving water
  • Stay tuned to live weather advisories and local radio
  • Disconnect main electrical switches if advised
  • Follow official evacuation orders without delay

After a Disaster

  • Return home only after authorities declare it safe
  • Avoid standing water and downed power lines
  • Inspect buildings for structural damage before entering
  • Document damages with photographs for relief assistance
  • Disinfect all water containers and living areas

Emergency Kit Checklist

Essential Supplies

  • Water (1 gallon per person per day for 3 days)
  • Non-perishable food (3-day supply)
  • Manual can opener and multi-tool
  • First aid kit and essential medications
  • Flashlight with spare batteries

Important Documents

  • Personal identification (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID)
  • Insurance policies and property records
  • Bank account and financial documents
  • Emergency contact numbers list
  • Physical local area evacuation map

Safety & Hygiene Items

  • Prescription medications and medical supplies
  • Hand sanitizer, soap, and sanitation supplies
  • Sturdy boots, gloves, and protective gear
  • Cell phone with power banks and charging cables
  • Cash in small denominations
